Lane: calm, trust-led consumer products that promise a better life for less effort.

## Five principles distilled

1. **Lead with the life, not the machine** (Oura, Function, Rise). Headline is about how life feels; the subhead names the mechanism in one concrete line.
2. **Specifics are the trust** (Function, Eight Sleep). Exact numbers, named objects, footnotes. Pre-launch Cerna has no reviews, so trust must come from precision about what it actually does (UBS PDF, Revolut CSV, CHF, duplicates) and from honest limits.
3. **Show the autopilot as a before/after object** (Eight Sleep sliders, Things screenshots). A small, legible artifact per beat beats a paragraph.
4. **Remove the fear at the button** (Sunsama "No credit card required", Things "a few times per year"). One line under the CTA that says exactly what happens to the email.
5. **Name the enemy, then go quiet** (Sunsama, Rise, Finch). One sharp line about the spreadsheet; everything else calm and sparse.

## What I deliberately did not take

Celebrity/expert endorsement walls, self-reported percentage stats, repeated carousels (Eight Sleep, Function repeat blocks), store-badge spam. Cerna has none of that evidence yet and faking it would contradict "honest over impressive".
