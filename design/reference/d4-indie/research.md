## Five principles taken

1. **The hero is the product's own artifact doing its job** (Granola, Tuple). For Cerna: a messy statement in a spreadsheet grid cleaning itself up.
2. **Turn the core claim into a picture** (Fathom's annotated cookie banner). "Duplicates removed" is shown as a struck row with its reason, not stated.
3. **Concentrate personality in one device** (Tally's marker swoosh). Here: the spreadsheet cell-selection rectangle, which becomes the email field ("the last cell you fill in").
4. **Earn trust with candour and refusals, not logos** (Plausible P.S., Buttondown commitments, Granola "without a bot", Screen Studio's stated limits). A short "what Cerna won't do" list and a signed founder note.
5. **Kill the doubt right under the button** (Fathom, Tally, Tuple microcopy). One line: what the email is for and what never happens with it.

Rejected: logo walls (Fathom/Screen Studio use them; Cerna has none and faking it breaks principle 4), scarcity theatre (Superhuman; only honest when real).
